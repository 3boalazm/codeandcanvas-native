Our Position

Business development delivered through technology and design — not despite them.

Most studios separate brand from technology. Most consultancies separate strategy from delivery. We built Code & Canvas to collapse those gaps — into a single studio, two specialist tracks, and a shared commercial discipline that makes both more effective.

01Why Code & Canvas

Not a generic studio.
Not a consultancy either.

Six things that make our engagements structurally different — not just "we care more."

01

Productized, not open-ended

Every engagement has defined scope, fixed milestones, and a traceable business outcome. Retainers are available only for specified services. No surprise scope creep. You know exactly what you're buying before signing.

02

BD logic in every track

Whether we're building a brand or an architecture, the starting point is always the same: your business goals. We use the Business Motivation Model before any design or tech decision is made.

03

Dual expertise — one studio

An Enterprise Architect and a Brand Identity Designer as co-founders means technology and brand decisions happen in the same room, aligned from day one.

04

Deep healthcare domain knowledge

CPHIMS, HIMSS EMRAM, CBAHI, NORA — active credentials, not listed frameworks. Every HC engagement is delivered with clinical workflow context built in from the first call.

05

MENA-native delivery

Bilingual AR/EN as standard. Vision 2030 and NORA alignment built into architecture. Saudi-specific regulatory context embedded in every technology recommendation.

06

Milestone-based payment

Payment is tied to deliverables, not time. Files release only on full settlement. This structure protects both sides and keeps every milestone meaningful.
