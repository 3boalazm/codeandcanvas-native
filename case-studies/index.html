01Healthcare · Multi-tenant SaaS · 2024–2026

صيدليتي Pharmacy OS

Doctor Pharmacy

A pharmacy chain running point of sale, stock, prescriptions and finance in separate tools, with no single view of a shift or a branch.

Constraints
  • Arabic RTL as the primary interface, not a translated skin
  • Multi-tenant from day one — branches must not see each other’s data
  • Must work on the counter hardware already in the branches
  • Pharmacy workflows: batch numbers, expiry and prescription linking
Our role

Architecture and full-stack delivery by the studio — data model, multi-tenant boundaries, RTL interface system and the module rollout order.

Approach
  • Modelled the pharmacy day first: counter, shift, stock receipt, prescription, month-end
  • One tenant-isolation rule enforced at the data layer, not in each screen
  • Shipped in modules, each one usable the week it was delivered
Artifacts
14 modules shippedPoint of sale, invoicing, inventory and goods receipt, prescriptions, customer records, shifts, reports
Multi-tenant data modelTenant isolation, branch roll-ups and per-branch permissions
Bilingual interface systemRTL-first components reused across every module
Next.jsNestJSTypeScriptPrismaPostgresPWA
Publication status & evidence

No public screenshots. Screens contain branch data, so any published view would need written client approval first.

02PWA · Offline-first · Public project · 2026

زاد Zad

Worship Companion

A daily-use worship companion for the first ten days of Dhul-Hijjah — used in places with weak or no connectivity.

Constraints
  • Fully usable offline — the offline state is the normal state
  • Bilingual Arabic/English with correct RTL presentation
  • Readable type for Quranic text at phone sizes
  • No account, no tracking, nothing to sign up for
Our role

Concept, interface design and front-end implementation, including the offline data strategy and type system.

Approach
  • Data cached on first load; every core feature works with the radio off
  • Arabic typography tested on a small screen before the language switch was built
  • Gesture and thumb reach drove the layout more than the desktop comp
Artifacts
12 worship modulesPrayer times, Qibla, Quran, adhkar, tasbih, Ruqyah, Asmaa Allah, Ramadan, Hajj, kids section
Offline-first data layerService worker and local storage — no network calls required for core reading
Public releaseDeployed publicly, open for anyone to use without an account
PWAService WorkerVanilla JSlocalStorageVercel
Publication status & evidence

The live app is the evidence — anyone can open it and use the offline state.

03Pharma ERP · Internal system · 2026

نايل فارما Nile Pharma ERP

Nile Pharma, Food & Supplements

A pharmaceutical distributor running the full sales cycle on paper and spreadsheets — including expiry-sensitive stock and rep incentives.

Constraints
  • FEFO allocation is compulsory, not a best effort
  • Consignment (amanah) stock must be tracked separately from owned stock
  • Rep incentives calculated from actual collections, not invoices issued
  • The team had to keep working during rollout — no downtime window
Our role

Business analysis, system design and delivery of the ERP modules with the client’s operations team.

Approach
  • Mapped the sales cycle end to end with the reps before designing any screen
  • FEFO enforced in the allocation engine so no screen can bypass it
  • Incentive rules written down as formulas and reviewed with finance
Artifacts
10 ERP modulesCatalogue, inventory, consignment, FEFO sales orders, invoicing, collections, incentives
Allocation engineFEFO rules applied to every order, with an audit trail
Incentive calculationRep ranking and payouts derived from collected amounts
React 18RTLIBM Plex Sans ArabicFEFO logic
Publication status & evidence

Nothing can be published: commercial volumes, margins and rep data are all under NDA. What can be discussed on a call is the design approach, not the numbers.
