02OUR PUBLICATION RULE

Evidence before wording.

Four rules applied to every number, certificate, testimonial and image on this site.

01

Dated source

A baseline, method, denominator, date and owner. Without them the number is not published.

02

Non-overlapping definition

A brand is counted once, sectors are classified, and founder experience is separated from the studio’s age.

03

An image is not evidence

Illustrative art is labelled, client screenshots need permission, business outcomes need measurement.

04

Remove or rewrite

If proof is unavailable, we rewrite into honest qualitative wording or remove the claim entirely.
