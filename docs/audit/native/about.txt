03 Our Methodology
8 stages. Every engagement.
From discovery to launch, every Code & Canvas engagement follows the same structured process — anchored in the Business Motivation Model and TOGAF ADM. Nothing is built without a business reason.
Payment is milestone-based — not time-based. Brand engagements: 50% Kickoff · 25% Strategy Sign-off · 25% Final. Advisory: 50% Kickoff · 50% Final. Files release on full settlement.
