HOW WE DECIDE · WHAT WE REFUSE

Judgement before craft.
Stated as decisions, not claims.

The hardest question to ask a studio is not “what do you do?” but “do you reason about my problem the way I do?”. This page answers it with four written decisions: what we would recommend, what we refuse to do, which published service carries it, and exactly what evidence exists today.

01FOUR DECISIONS

The situation, the decision, and the refusal.

Each block is the shape of a real conversation: how we read the situation, what we would actually do, what we will not do, and which published engagement delivers it.

01

INTEGRATION

Our systems don’t talk to each other

Three vendors, no single owner, and a monthly report that is retyped by hand. The instinct is to buy another tool; the cause is that no one owns the data contract between the tools you already have.

What we would decide

Draw the value stream and the data contracts first, then decide what to integrate, replace or retire — in that order, with the cost of each written down.

What we refuse

We will not start by demoing a platform. A tool decision made before the data model is a decision someone will pay for twice.

  • AB-04Solution ArchitectureCustom scope
02

HEALTHCARE

We’re choosing an HIS or an ERP

Every vendor demo looks the same, and nobody owns the feasibility study or the risk register. The choice is usually made on the demo, then justified for two years.

What we would decide

Score the vendors against your own clinical workflows and the readiness criteria you are actually judged by (EMRAM stage, CBAHI readiness, NORA interoperability), then negotiate from a written gap report.

What we refuse

We do not take a commission from any vendor, and we do not run a selection we are not willing to lose.

Published services that carry it
  • HC-03HIS Selection Advisory$2,500 – $5,000
  • HC-05Healthcare EA Assessment$1,500 – $3,500
03

BRAND

Our brand doesn’t match our ambition

The logo is fine; the system behind it is missing. Every new deck, post and event re-decides the same things, so the brand drifts while the business grows.

What we would decide

Position first, then a system a team can operate without us: type, grid, colour, tone, and the rules that keep Arabic and English consistent.

What we refuse

We will not sell a logo sprint as a brand system, and we will not deliver a guideline nobody can apply on a Tuesday.

Published services that carry it
  • MA-02Brand Identity Essentials$640 – $1,065
  • MA-03Full Brand System$1,490 – $2,975+
04

LAUNCH

We’re launching and need both

A new clinic, product or market entry needs identity and a working digital front at the same time — usually with one budget and one date that cannot slip.

What we would decide

One brief, one timeline, two owners: brand and build run in the same weeks, with a single hand-over pack instead of two vendors blaming each other.

What we refuse

We will not take a launch date we cannot hold, and we will not hide a dependency inside “phase two”.

Published services that carry it
  • CC-01Digital Launch Package$3,500 – $7,000+
  • HC-01Clinic Digital Launch$3,500 – $7,000+
