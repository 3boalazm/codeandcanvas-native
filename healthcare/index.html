03REGULATORY CONTEXT

The vocabulary the decisions are made in.

These are the frameworks and standards the work is delivered against. Anything project-specific — including which requirements apply to your facility — is confirmed with your quality and compliance leads before scope is fixed.

CBAHI

Saudi central board for accreditation of healthcare institutions — the accreditation body clinic and hospital work is measured against in KSA.

NORA

The national health information exchange architecture — the interoperability frame KSA healthcare integrations are expected to follow.

HL7 · FHIR · DICOM

The standards used to move clinical data between HIS, LIS, RIS/PACS and portals without rebuilding them every time.

ETA e-invoice (EG)

Egyptian tax authority e-invoicing requirements, relevant to clinic billing and pharmacy workflows in Egypt.

We do not claim regulatory accreditation or certification for your facility. The engagement produces an assessment, documents and a roadmap; the decision and regulatory responsibility stay with you and your compliance owners. Discuss the detail.
