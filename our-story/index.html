03Healthcare Vertical

ILLUSTRATIVE · NOT CLIENT WORK 03 HC · Clinical Architecture Clinical readiness · interoperability

Where clinical context is built in from day one.

Clinical context from the first call — not briefed in on the first call. Readiness criteria, interoperability and accreditation gaps are part of the first conversation, not a later phase.

Healthcare IT is where a wrong decision costs most — a misaligned HIS is not a licence you cancel, it is years of workarounds and manual reporting. We operate inside the ecosystem: clinical workflow context in every technology recommendation, CBAHI-aware design in every visual decision.

04Data & Analytics

Where data stops being a reporting burden.

Where operational data starts being the thing that tells leadership what to do next.

The problem is rarely the data — it is the distance between the data and the decision. We close it by building the right KPI framework first, then connecting the data, then making it visible in a format leadership can act on without a data team in the room.
