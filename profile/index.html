A small studio with large decisions.

Code & Canvas is a business-development studio working at the intersection of brand, product and technology. We enter at the moment a decision gets expensive — a new position, a new product, a new system — and leave with something that runs, not a slide deck.

02WHAT WE BELIEVE

Six convictions that govern every project.

Not marketing lines — operating rules that explain why we decline some projects, why we slow down some phases, and why we deliver less than some expect and more than others need.

01

Business first, then beauty

Beauty that doesn’t sell is not design; it is an expense. We start with the economics of the offer before we choose the typeface.

02

One team, no lost hand-off

Strategist, writer, designer and engineer in the same room — or the same file. No translation lost between agency and dev shop.

03

Published scope, before the contract

Every service has a published scope, duration and price band. No hidden hours, no “it depends” halfway through.

04

AI is leverage, not theatre

We apply automation where it saves time or prevents error. If there is no real need, we advise you not to build it.

05

Proof before the promise

We publish numbers and live sites, not slogans. Every build in this profile can be opened and verified.

06

We say no early

If the problem is solved by an off-the-shelf template, we will tell you. A profitable wrong project costs more than an honest no.

06THE METHOD

Four phases. No surprises.

We work in clear weekly rhythms: a weekly demo, one shared file and logged decisions. At any moment you know where the project is and what it needs from you.

01

Discover

A 90-minute session, a written brief and a scope you approve before any financial commitment.

Output: signed scope + timeline
02

Define

Positioning, requirements and success metrics. No pixels before this is agreed.

Output: strategy / spec
03

Design & Build

Weekly demo, one shared file, logged decisions. Design and build move together.

Output: working increments
04

Deploy & Handover

Live launch, training, documentation and a 30-day post-handover support window.

Output: live system + handover pack

What we need from you

One decision-maker, access to internal knowledge, and feedback within 48 hours. That is all.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell open-ended hours, we don’t start execution without scope, and we don’t hold source files hostage — they are yours from day one.
