Inquiries & bookings

Sending a form creates a lead record; booking a call creates a reservation with a reference and a calendar file. A confirmation is shown only after the server accepts the request. If the service is unavailable, the site says the request was not sent and offers a manual channel. We may decline or reschedule a call; no fee is charged for a discovery call. The call is not a contract and no deliverable is promised by booking it.
