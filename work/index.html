03HOW REAL WORK GETS PUBLISHED

From the brief to the evidence.

The gate a real client case must pass before it replaces — or sits beside — a concept study.

01 · PERMISSION

Permission

Written client permission naming what may be shown, in which markets and for how long.

02 · EVIDENCE

Evidence

Approved screenshots or photography, with people, health and financial data redacted.

03 · ATTRIBUTION

Attribution

The studio’s exact role, the team, the date and the constraints — no borrowed credit.
