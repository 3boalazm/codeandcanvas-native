01THE CASE STRUCTURE

Every project is told with the same honesty.

Problem, strategy, our role, execution and an outcome that states exactly what is — and is not — proven.

01 · PROBLEM

The problem

A company site lists 20 services with equal weight, hides its work three clicks down and offers four competing buttons above the fold.

02 · STRATEGY

The strategy

Choose one commercial promise, prove it with work, then give the visitor exactly one next step and one honest secondary path.

03 · OUR ROLE

Our role

Strategy, information architecture, interface design and measurement specification — produced by the studio as a method study, with no client commission.

02EXECUTION

The steps that actually ship.

Concrete deliverables, not adjectives. Each line is something a client can open, review and keep.

Execution steps

  • Rewrite the first screen around the outcome the buyer wants, not the studio philosophy
  • Move selected work and labelled evidence above the service catalogue
  • Reduce the primary CTA to one; keep the secondary as a low-commitment read
  • Instrument service_view → cta_click → form_start → form_submit → booking_confirmed

What you keep

  • Message hierarchy
  • Proof map
  • CTA spec
  • Measurement plan
